# Eval Case: Low-Material Student Mining

## Purpose

Test whether the skill helps a student who feels they have little resume material by mining small truthful experiences instead of inventing achievements or giving up.

## Prompt

```text
Use $high-density-resume 帮我看看简历能写什么。

我大一，没实习没竞赛，也没什么项目。平时就参加社团活动，帮老师和同学处理过一些小事，会一点 Excel，也学过中医针灸基础，偶尔帮同学做基础放松。目标还不确定，可能想找办公室助理、运营助理或者普通实习。
```

## Expected Behavior

- Identify this as low-material mining, not ordinary resume rewriting.
- Ask concrete follow-up questions about coursework, activity roles, micro-tasks, tools, helping behavior, and target direction.
- Translate small experiences into enterprise value such as execution reliability, communication cost reduction, team trust, service mindset, organizing ability, and memorable scarcity.
- Treat acupuncture as a low-priority distinctive signal only if factual and interview-defensible.
- Avoid medical claims such as treatment, cure, diagnosis, or guaranteed improvement.
- Avoid drafting a full resume before enough facts exist.
