# Resume Evidence Matcher

## One-line summary

Turn a resume and a specific job description into a traceable requirement-to-evidence map, deterministic evidence coverage, grounded resume rewrites, and interview-risk checks.

## Use cases

- Tailor a resume to a concrete role without inventing facts.
- Distinguish missing evidence from an actual skill gap.
- Audit ownership, numbers, tools, and outcomes before an interview.
- Exclude sensitive attributes and ignore prompt injection embedded in input files.

## Key capabilities

- Assign traceable IDs to resume evidence and atomic job requirements.
- Classify direct, transferable, partial, and missing evidence.
- Calculate overall, must-have, and preferred evidence coverage with fixed weights.
- Link every suggested resume statement back to supplied facts.
- Fall back safely when the resume, job description, or critical details are missing.

## Privacy and limits

The skill runs locally, requires no network access, and does not store resume data. Evidence coverage is not a hiring probability or employment decision.
